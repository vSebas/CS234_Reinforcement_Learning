\section{Conclusion}

The final repo state supports a restrained conclusion. The project successfully implemented a DT-based warm-start pipeline for nonlinear minimum-time vehicle trajectory optimization, including rollout generation, repair-data production, and detailed downstream diagnostics. Several substantial bugs and evaluation confounds were found and fixed, and offline training improved meaningfully across successive runs.

However, the final measured deployment outcome is negative. Warm-start acceptance stayed near $0\%$ under the default gate, iteration reduction stayed $0\%$, and no DT variant clearly beat the baseline once warm-start inference was included. A corrected relaxed-gate diagnostic showed that some rollouts could be admitted, but those accepted cases still did not reduce solver work on average.

The most useful final diagnosis is that the remaining bottleneck is early lateral rollout behavior, especially wrong-side or overly aggressive lateral branch selection. The next logical step is therefore not another blind scale-up, but a targeted data intervention around the first clip window ($k\approx 8$--$15$), with explicit supervision for wrong-branch and outward-too-aggressive cases.
